---

Using the team already discussed in this session, explain the team core in depth:

**Win Condition** — What is the primary path to victory? (e.g. "Calyrex-Ice under Trick Room sweeps everything after Incineroar gets Intimidate + Parting Shot")

**Core Pokemon** — Which 2-4 Pokemon form the backbone? Why do they belong together?

**Synergy Breakdown** — For each pair in the core:
- What does Pokemon A give Pokemon B?
- What does Pokemon B give Pokemon A?
- What threats do they cover together that neither handles alone?

**Support Shell** — How do the remaining 2-4 Pokemon enable the core?
- Who sets speed control (tailwind/TR)?
- Who provides redirection or chip damage?
- Who handles the core's biggest counters?

**Weaknesses of the Core** — What can shut down this win condition? What does the team need to watch out for?

If no team has been discussed yet in this session, ask the user to paste their team first.
